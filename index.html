    WHAT IS THIS?

    Maze Lab is a game about how computers find their way.

    When your phone plans a route, or a video-game enemy chases you round a corner, a pathfinding algorithm is at work. That's a step-by-step recipe for getting from A to B. A computer can't see the whole map at once like you can. It has to explore one spot at a time.

    Each fighter here is one of those recipes. They all start at the same place and hunt for the same goal, but they search in very different ways. Watch them, read their hints, and see who's quick, who's careful, and who's just lucky.

    HOW TO PLAY

    1. Pick a fighter from the roster.
    2. Pick a level, or click and drag on the board to draw your own walls.
    3. Add slime tiles. They cost 5 to cross instead of 1.
    4. Press START and watch. Turn on HINT to have your fighter explain every move.
    5. In VS BATTLE, pit 2 to 4 fighters against each other and enter your initials if you set a high score.

    TILE KEY

    • START where your fighter begins
    • GOAL the flag it's hunting for
    • WALL can't go through
    • SLIME costs 5 to step in
    • NEXT UP tiles it plans to check
    • EXPLORED tiles already checked
    • PATH the coin trail it found
